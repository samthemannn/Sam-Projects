Option 2: A market trend analysis.
 

 

Context

Your mission is to select a trend that interests you and is connected to your career goals and conduct a comprehensive analysis. This project aims to develop your ability to:

Synthesize data from various credible sources
Form a coherent narrative about a trend’s past, present, and potential future
Apply critical thinking to complex concepts
Present findings in a clear, engaging manner
 

Instructions

An effective market trend analysis should include the following four sections.

 

Trend Definition and Background
Begin your investigation by defining your chosen trend.

Dive into academic databases and industry publications as outlined in the reading
Construct a thorough explanation of what the trend entails
Explain why it matters in today’s business landscape
Consider the key technologies or principles driving this trend

Current Relevance and Impact
Delve into the current significance of your chosen trend.

Present at least 5-7 solid data points or examples
Include quantitative and quantitative information from market research reports
Explore any controversies or challenges associated with the trend
 

Industry Adoption Analysis
Focus on how various industries are adopting this trend.

Identify 3-4 sectors where the trend is being adopted
Find specific examples of companies implementing the trend
Analyze the results of these implementations
Uncover an unexpected industry adopting the trend in an innovative way

Future Trajectory Forecast
Conclude with a forecast of the trend’s future development.

Predict how the trend might evolve over the next 3-5 years
Consider emerging technologies or cultural shifts that could influence its trajectory
Demonstrate your ability to synthesize information and draw meaningful conclusions
 

Writing your Report

When presenting your research:

Organize your findings using clear, logical paragraphs
Use subheadings to delineate major sections of your analysis
Incorporate and explain 3-4 relevant charts, graphs, or infographics
The final report should be approximately 1000 words
Ensure proper citation of all sources
Include a bibliography or works cited section
 

AI Usage

Once you have written the Current Relevance and Impact section of the paper, use AI to develop the Future Trajectory Forecast. Using the prompt “based on the attached information generate at least three possible future forecasts for the trend.” You should review the predictions generated by artificial intelligence and determine the most likely future and use this information to generate the Future Trajectory Forecast section of the paper. Any information that is copied directly from AI should be placed in quotation marks. 


Conclusion

Remember, the goal of this project isn’t just to collect information, but to demonstrate your ability to analyze, synthesize, and draw meaningful conclusions from complex data. These skills are the foundation of effective marketing strategies and will serve you well in your future career.
