# Gemini Frontend Contract
Build a desktop-first React/TypeScript interface with screens for discovery, search, book detail, series detail, rating editor, reviews, profiles, shelves, recommendations, and authentication.
Show the user's own rating separately from the public score. Always show rating count. Provide category controls without requiring them. Hide spoiler reviews behind an explicit reveal action. Include loading, empty, error, and success states. Do not duplicate score calculations in the frontend.
